Our story

About Glow & Co.

Glow & Co. was born from a simple idea: a salon should feel like a refuge, not a chore. We wanted to create a space where people could slow down, be themselves, and leave feeling genuinely renewed — not just styled.

Founded in 2021, we started as a two-chair studio with a big dream. Today, our team of 12 stylists, colorists, and skincare specialists serves thousands of clients each year, all while keeping that same intimate, personal feel.

Every detail — from our rose-tinted interiors to our curated playlist — is designed to make you feel at home. No rushed appointments, no upselling. Just great hair, glowing skin, and a moment of calm.
