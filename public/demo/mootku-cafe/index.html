Some names are made up. Some names mean everything.

“Mootku” began as a loving nickname — a little word that carries affection, warmth and togetherness. That feeling grew into a café and resto where friends meet, families gather and ordinary days find a reason to celebrate.

It's not just about what's on the plate. It's about the people around the table, the stories that stretch past dessert, and a place that feels a little like yours.
